Vinod Pudushery Anthony
Financial Adviser
Vinod has called Dunedin home for more than 12 years and brings a diverse
background in financial services, business leadership and people management to his
role at Wealthify Mortgage Advisors.
He holds a Master of Social Work and the New Zealand Certificate in Financial
Services (Level 5). Before becoming a Financial Adviser, Vinod gained hands-on
industry experience as a Mortgage Support Officer. He has also worked as a
company director and Human Resources Manager, giving him a broad
understanding of business, employment and the financial challenges faced by
individuals and families.
Vinod can assist with first-home purchases, refinancing, investment properties,
construction lending and a wide range of mortgage needs. He believes financial
advice should be clear, practical and tailored to each client. By taking the time to
understand their circumstances and goals, Vinod helps clients explore suitable
options and supports them throughout the journey—from the first conversation
through to approval and settlement.
Friendly, approachable and committed to excellent service, Vinod is passionate
about helping people make informed financial decisions and move forward with
confidence.
Vinod speaks English, Malayalam, Hindi, Kannada and Tamil, enabling him to
connect with and support clients from diverse communities.
