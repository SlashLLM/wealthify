What It Looks At

Six areas, one honest picture

Your adviser looks across your whole financial life — not just the part you came in about — so the advice you get fits the situation you're actually in.

Cashflow & budget

Whether there's money left at the end of the month, where it's quietly going, and which bills or subscriptions are worth a second look.

Debt & repayments

How your mortgage is structured, what your rate and refix date mean for you, and whether any other debt is costing more than it needs to.

Safety buffer

What happens if the car dies or income stops for a month — how much cash you can reach quickly, and how long it would carry you.

Protection

Whether the cover you have matches the risks your household actually faces — and where the gaps sit if the worst happened.

Future wealth

Your KiwiSaver contributions and fund fit, your saving habits, and whether you're tracking toward the goals you've set for yourself.

Financial readiness

Whether your paperwork, renewal dates and goals are in order — so nothing important sneaks up on you unnoticed.
