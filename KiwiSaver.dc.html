Fund Types

Find your fund

Your fund type has a big impact on your KiwiSaver. It's worth checking whether yours still fits your age and goals.

Conservative
~3–4% p.a. net
Low risk High risk
Mostly cash and bonds. Low volatility but low growth. Suitable when retirement is very close — within 2–3 years.
Typically suits: Retiring soon
Balanced
~5–6% p.a. net
Low risk High risk
Mix of shares and bonds. Moderate growth with some volatility buffer. Good middle ground for those 10+ years from retirement.
Typically suits: 10–15 yrs to retire
Growth
~7–8% p.a. net
Low risk High risk
Mostly shares — global and NZ equities. Stronger long-term return potential, with more short-term market swings. May suit those with a longer time horizon.
Typically suits: 15+ yrs to retire
Aggressive
~8–10% p.a. net
Low risk High risk
100% high-growth equities. Maximum long-term potential but significant swings. Requires a high tolerance for volatility. May suit those with a long time horizon and high risk tolerance.
Typically suits: 20+ yrs, higher risk tolerance
Past returns don't guarantee future performance. Returns are indicative averages across NZ KiwiSaver funds. A KiwiSaver specialist can help you choose based on your individual situation.
